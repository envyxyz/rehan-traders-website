## 3. People Also Ask (PAA) & Chatbot Knowledge Base (27 Questions)

### Screen & Glass Replacement (OCA Lamination)
1. **Can I change only the outer glass if my display and touch are working?**  
   *Answer:* Yes. Using OCA (Optically Clear Adhesive) vacuum lamination machines at Rehan Traders (Shop G-88), we separate cracked outer glass and laminate new factory glass while keeping your 100% original OLED/Super AMOLED panel.
2. **What is the price difference between glass replacement and a full screen assembly?**  
   *Answer:* Glass replacement usually costs 60%–75% less than buying a brand-new original screen assembly.
3. **Will my touch sensitivity or in-display fingerprint sensor be affected after glass change?**  
   *Answer:* No. Because the underlying original panel is preserved, touch response, refresh rate (120Hz), and fingerprint sensor accuracy remain completely intact.
4. **How do I know if my phone needs just glass or a complete screen replacement?**  
   *Answer:* If there are colored vertical lines, black ink bleeding spots, screen flickering, or total blackness, the inner panel is dead and requires a full display replacement. If the image is clear and touch works, only glass is replaced.
5. **How long does touch glass replacement take at Hafeez Center?**  
   *Answer:* On average, 1 to 2.5 hours, including laser separation, glue cleaning, OCA lamination, and bubble removing.

### D. Crucial Rule: Google's Self-Serving Review Policy
*   **The Policy:** In September 2019, Google updated review snippet guidelines: `AggregateRating` and `Review` markup embedded on a site's own `LocalBusiness` or `Organization` pages are deemed **self-serving**.
*   **What this means for Rehan Traders:**
    *   Do **NOT** hardcode `AggregateRating` (e.g. 4.9 stars / 120 reviews) inside the `LocalBusiness` schema on `rehanmobiletraders.com`. Google will simply ignore it and will **not** display golden star rich snippets in search results.
    *   Third-party review widgets attempting to output local business review schema will also be disregarded.
    *   **The Solution:** Stars in local map packs and organic local results stem exclusively from your verified **Google Business Profile (GBP)**. Embed text testimonials on the site for human trust, but leave the rating schema off the local entity code.
